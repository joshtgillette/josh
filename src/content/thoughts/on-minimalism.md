---
title: On keeping it small
date: 2026-06-08
description: A short note on why less chrome usually wins.
---

Every element on a page is a small tax on attention. The default should be to
leave things out — fewer borders, fewer boxes, fewer decisions for the eye to
make. What remains gets to matter more.

That's the whole idea behind this place: text, a little space, and not much else.
